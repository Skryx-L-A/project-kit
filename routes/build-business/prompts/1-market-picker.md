# Prompt 1: Market picker (Day 1)

Use a Claude session with web research enabled and attach `rubrics/market-criteria.md`.
The result is a shortlist of three markets, each scored against the rubric.

> Act as a research analyst helping a newcomer choose the niche for a solo automation
> business that serves local companies. Investigate several candidate niches and present a
> shortlist of three. Judge every niche on four tests, using the attached rubric:
> (1) others already earn well selling similar services there, (2) at least 10,000 potential
> customers exist, (3) customers pay large amounts per engagement, (4) the niche is expanding.
> For each niche name some providers that already succeed in it, estimate the customer count,
> give typical prices, and describe the trend. Close with a ranking and a single
> recommendation.
>
> About me:
> - Background in any field: {{EXPERIENCE_or_"none"}}
> - People I know who run or work in a business: {{CONNECTIONS_or_"none"}}
> - Money available for tools and software: {{BUDGET_e.g._$5,000}}
>
> The shortlist must contain three niches, no more and no fewer.

**Notes:** there is no flawless niche, so aim for a solid one and decide quickly. Entering a
niche nobody has proven yet is a risk, not an advantage. Niches with repeat customers, big
invoices and visible demand (aesthetic clinics, dentistry, heating and cooling trades,
longevity and hormone clinics) tend to rank well.
