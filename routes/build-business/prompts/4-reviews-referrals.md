# Prompt 4: Reviews and referrals (Day 4, service 2)

> Draft all messages for a review-and-referral program for the local business below, each in
> an SMS version and an email version. The program runs like this:
> 1. Message former and current customers, announce a prize draw ({{RAFFLE e.g. $1,000 gift
>    card or one year of free membership}}), and ask them to rate their visit from 1 to 5.
> 2. Ratings of 4 or 5 receive a message with the link to the public Google review page.
> 3. Ratings of 1 to 3 go to a private feedback form instead, never to Google; thank them and
>    ask what should be improved.
> 4. Once someone has posted a public review, send an AI-written thank-you and straight away
>    invite them to refer friends through a personal link. Every friend who signs up gives
>    the referrer five to ten extra draw entries.
>
> - Niche: {{MARKET}}
> - Business name: {{BUSINESS_NAME}}

**Why:** nearly all shoppers read reviews first, a rise from 3.5 to 4.0 stars is associated
with a revenue gain of roughly 5 to 10 percent, and few businesses ever ask. People are most
willing to refer someone right after they have said something nice. A steady flow of recent,
high ratings also helps local search results. (These figures are commonly quoted marketing
numbers; treat them as unverified.)

**Compliance:** same consent and opt-out rules as for reactivation. Do not condition prizes on
positive ratings or buy reviews, which Google forbids; the draw rewards sharing an honest
opinion, whatever it says.
