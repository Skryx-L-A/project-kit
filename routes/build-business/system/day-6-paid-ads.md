# Day 6: Win clients through ads
**Aim:** get qualified owners to approach you. Cold messaging at volume is easy for everyone
and therefore ignored, so publish ads patterned on ones that already work.

**Steps:**
1. Browse the free Facebook Ad Library for long-running competitor ads in your niche.
2. Run `prompts/6-ad-analyzer.md` with the screenshots to get the shared patterns and three
   new ads.
3. Save the result in `acquisition/ads.md`. Produce the visuals with an image tool you have
   (for example the higgsfield skills), since Claude cannot draw pictures.
4. Record the budget, the targeting and the offer used in each ad.

**Deliverable:** `acquisition/ads.md`.
