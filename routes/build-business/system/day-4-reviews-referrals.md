# Day 4: Service 2, reviews and referrals
**Aim:** build the second service, an automation that collects reviews (prize draw plus
routing by rating) and converts satisfied customers into referrals.

**Steps:**
1. Run `prompts/4-reviews-referrals.md` with the prize you chose.
2. Keep the text and email versions in `pillars/reviews-referrals.md`.
3. Set up the routing: ratings 4 and 5 go to the Google review link, ratings 1 to 3 to a
   private form, and after a published review the customer gets a thank-you and a referral
   invitation (personal link, extra draw entries).
4. Compliance: reward honest feedback rather than good ratings, and observe consent and
   opt-out rules.

**Deliverable:** `pillars/reviews-referrals.md`.
