# Day 2: Pick a target network
**Aim:** narrow the niche to a group whose members recommend you onward. Prefer franchise
chains and similar networks over scattered independents: one strong result can turn into many
warm introductions.

**Steps:**
1. Run `prompts/2-ship-finder.md` to get ten real chains with store counts and growth.
2. Choose one to three chains and research their largest franchisees.
3. Carry out the Facebook introduction routine described in that prompt file (finding owners
   through shared connections and using your profile as a shop window).

**Deliverable:** `SHIP.md`.
