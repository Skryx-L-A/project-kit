# Go-to-market, outreach and conversion
Inspired by public founder-skill collections (see `SOURCES.md`); the text below is written
independently.

Apply:
- **Go-to-market planning:** draft a few alternative acquisition strategies and pick one.
- **Outreach:** a set of message templates; a warm introduction beats a cold message, which
  supports the Day 2 routine.
- **Competitor research:** check claims against evidence and anticipate likely moves; feeds the
  ad analysis in Prompt 6.
- **Conversion optimisation:** a short list of principles for improving the client's booking
  funnel.
- **Pricing, hooks and lead magnets,** and a checklist for a launch on a product-discovery site.
- **Copy structures:** AIDA, PAS and BAB, plus templates for social posts.

Further ideas worth checking in other public collections: the full marketing lifecycle (copy,
conversion, search, launch, pricing); positioning frameworks such as Jobs-to-be-Done,
StoryBrand and scorecard marketing; SaaS financial projections (revenue multiples, EBITDA,
discounted cash flow); a landing-page method built on PAS with a scored conversion checklist;
and Anthropic's published presentation and skill-authoring skills.

Use for client acquisition, landing pages and positioning beyond the basic seven-day plan.
